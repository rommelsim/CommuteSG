# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 May 2026

This policy describes what information the Commute iOS app collects, how it is used, and your rights. Commute is built and operated by an independent developer (contact below).

## Information collected

**Location.** Commute requests "While Using the App" location access to find bus stops and MRT stations near you. Your coordinates stay on your device and are used only to filter and sort nearby stops. Location is never sent to a server we operate.

**Saved preferences.** The app stores the following on your device only: saved places (Home, Work, custom destinations), favourite bus services and MRT stations, color scheme preference, and onboarding completion state. This data does not leave your device and is removed when you delete the app.

**No personal information.** Commute does not ask for, collect, or transmit personally identifying information such as your name, email, phone number, payment details, or account credentials. There is no account to create.

## Third-party services

**LTA DataMall.** Commute fetches transit data (bus stops, bus arrivals, train service alerts) from the Singapore Land Transport Authority's DataMall API. Requests include a developer-issued account key and the bus stop or station identifier you are looking up. No information about you is included. LTA's privacy practices are governed by their own policy, available at https://www.lta.gov.sg.

**Apple Maps (MapKit).** Map tiles and the location framework are provided by Apple. Their use is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

## Analytics, tracking, and advertising

Commute contains no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers, and no advertising. The app does not use the IDFA, does not track you across other apps or websites, and is not subject to App Tracking Transparency prompts.

## Data retention

Saved preferences remain on your device until you delete them in the app or remove the app from your device. There is no remote copy.

Cached LTA data (the list of bus stops and recent arrivals) is stored on your device for performance and refreshed periodically. It is also removed with the app.

## Children's privacy

Commute is suitable for all ages. Because the app does not collect personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Your rights

You can clear all saved data at any time by removing the app from your device. You can also reset onboarding from the Profile screen and re-complete it without saving any places.

## Changes to this policy

If material changes are made, the effective date at the top will be updated. The current version is always available at this URL.
